New CoMaps-based offline maps with Navi layers: smaller regional downloads, contour lines and experimental 3D, and improved place search. Existing map downloads can be migrated in-app (Wi-Fi by default; old data removed only after you confirm). GPX import is supported. This is the first mainline MWM-default release; earlier 0.4.0-beta and 0.4.1-beta labels were CAT-line builds (separate from this app), and an earlier draft reused the 0.4.0-beta name by mistake. Vehicle size limits are not applied on the new maps yet; long regional routes may take about ten seconds to compute.
