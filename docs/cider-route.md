# The Norwegian Cider Route (Siderveien)

A suggested road-trip route connecting Norway's `brewery=cider` producers as
mapped on OpenStreetMap, running the length of the country from Rogaland in
the south to Trondheim in the north. Distances between the southern tip
(Fosmoen) and the northern tip (Sidergården) span roughly 5 degrees of
latitude — this is a multi-region trip best split into legs (Ryfylke/Rogaland
→ Hardanger → Sogn → Møre og Romsdal → Trøndelag), not a single day's drive.

Use this list to plan stops, generate a day-by-day itinerary, or build a map
route in Navi. All coordinates and names are sourced from OpenStreetMap
nodes/ways tagged `brewery=cider`.

This is a **suggested example**, not a built-in saved route in the app.

## Using this in Navi

1. Download the covering **map regions** in **Tools** (plus basemap if you
   want the map picture offline). The planner uses a **single** Geofabrik
   extract per plan and does **not** stitch two landsdels into one trip —
   split the corridor into legs, or you will get **Map data needed**. See
   README [Leaving a downloaded region](../README.md#leaving-a-downloaded-region).
2. For each day's From / Via / To, paste coordinates from the table as
   `lat, lon` in **Place** search (same format as
   [How to use Navi](how-to-use.md)). Save named places if you want to reuse
   stops.
3. **Plan route** per leg (Car is the usual mode). Hardanger/Sogn often
   involve ferries; **Avoid ferries** will force long inland detours.

Suggested Geofabrik extracts for the corridor:

| Leg | Stops | Geofabrik path |
|---|---|---|
| Southern Rogaland / Ryfylke | 1–6 | `europe/norway/sorlandet` and/or `europe/norway/vestlandet` (bboxes overlap here) |
| Hardanger, Sogn, most of Møre og Romsdal | 8–20, 22–29 | `europe/norway/vestlandet` |
| Fjordsider to Sidergården | 30–31 | `europe/norway/trondelag` |
| Detours (off the west-coast corridor) | 7 Egge Gård, 21 Bugarden | `europe/norway/ostlandet` |

## Suggested direction

Start at **either end**, depending on your trip:

- **South → North**: begin at Fosmoen Frukt & Cider (Rogaland) and finish at
  Sidergården (Trondheim) — good if arriving via Stavanger/Kristiansand.
- **North → South**: begin at Sidergården (Trondheim) and finish at Fosmoen
  Frukt & Cider (Rogaland) — good if arriving via Trondheim airport (Værnes).

The stop order below is listed south to north; reverse it for the
north-to-south direction.

## Notes

- Many stops are marked `shop=alcohol` on OSM, meaning direct sale of cider
  on-site — worth confirming opening hours before visiting, as several are
  small farms (`shop=farm`) with limited hours.
- Stops 7 (Egge Gård) and 21 (Bugarden) sit off the main Hardanger/Sogn
  corridor — geographically they are detours rather than waypoints on the
  direct coastal route.
- The heaviest concentration is in **Hardanger** (13 producers within a
  small radius) — this region alone could be its own dedicated day trip or
  weekend.
- Data pulled from OpenStreetMap elements tagged `brewery=cider`; some names
  were added/corrected via direct edits during route research and may not
  yet be reflected in all OSM-based apps until they resync.

## Suggested next steps

- Group stops into daily legs based on driving distance/ferry crossings
  (many Hardanger/Sogn routes involve fjord crossings).
- Cross-reference opening hours and tasting-room availability before
  finalizing dates, since farm shops often have seasonal hours.
